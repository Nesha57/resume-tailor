TECHVISTA SOLUTIONS - HR POLICY DOCUMENT
========================================

1. INTRODUCTION
This document outlines the standard HR policies for all employees at TechVista Solutions.

2. WORKING HOURS AND LOCATION
- Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Work From Home (WFH) Policy: Employees are eligible for up to 2 days of WFH per week, subject to manager approval.
- WFH days cannot be rolled over to the next week.

3. LEAVE POLICY
- Annual Leave: 20 days per calendar year.
- Sick Leave: 10 days per calendar year (medical certificate required for >3 consecutive days).
- Casual Leave: 5 days per calendar year.
- Leave Requests: All leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leaves.

4. PROBATION PERIOD
- All new hires are subject to a 3-month probation period.
- During probation, notice period is 15 days on either side.
- Benefits such as Annual Leave accrue during probation but can only be availed after successful confirmation.

5. NOTICE PERIOD (POST-PROBATION)
- The standard notice period for confirmed employees is 60 days (2 months).
- In exceptional cases, notice period buyout may be permitted at management's discretion.

6. CODE OF CONDUCT
- Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times.
- Confidentiality of company data and client information is strictly enforced.
