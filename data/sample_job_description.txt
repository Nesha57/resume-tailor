Job Title: Software Engineer
Company: TechVista Solutions
Location: Remote / Hybrid
Department: Engineering

About Us:
TechVista Solutions is a fast-growing tech company specializing in scalable web applications and AI-driven solutions.

Role Overview:
We are looking for a passionate Software Engineer to join our backend development team. You will be responsible for designing, building, and maintaining high-performance APIs and microservices.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop and maintain robust REST APIs and services.
- Write clean, testable, and efficient code.
- Collaborate with frontend teams to integrate APIs.
- Optimize application performance and database queries.
- Participate in code reviews and architectural discussions.

Required Skills & Qualifications:
- 1 to 3 years of professional experience in software development.
- Strong proficiency in Python and related frameworks (FastAPI, Django, or Flask).
- Experience with building and consuming REST APIs.
- Solid understanding of SQL and relational databases (PostgreSQL or MySQL).
- Proficiency with Git and version control workflows.
- Familiarity with basic frontend technologies (React, HTML/CSS) is a plus.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field.
